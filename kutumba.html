People & services
Each contact type has its own form — doctor, lawyer, CA, builder, home service, business
Each type has its own form
A doctor form captures conditions they manage, emergency number, and clinic timings. A lawyer form captures case history and documents they hold. A CA form covers scope, files held, and annual deadlines. A builder form records past projects and your preferences they know. Choose the right type and get exactly the right fields.
🩺 Doctor⚖️ Lawyer📊 CA / Accountant🔧 Home service🏗️ Builder / Architect🤝 Business contact
Health & genetics
Conditions, allergies, genetic risks — each type has its own specific form
Each type has its own form
An allergy form asks for triggers, reaction severity, EpiPen details, and emergency protocol. A chronic condition form tracks medication, monitoring schedule, and dietary restrictions. A genetic risk form spans both family sides and gives screening guidance for descendants. A medication entry captures dosage, frequency, and what not to combine with.
💊 Chronic condition⚠️ Allergy🧬 Genetic risk💉 Medication🥗 Nutrition pattern🏃 Lifestyle habit
Culture & celebrations
Festival, recipe, song, prayer, ritual — each gets a completely different form
Each type has its own form
A recipe form has ingredients with exact quantities, step-by-step method, and the secret tips that make it taste like home. A song form has lyrics in the original language, transliteration, meaning, and the memory attached. A festival form captures the full day-by-day sequence, who gathers, what is worn, and what is cooked. A prayer form preserves the exact words in the original language.

Stories & wisdom
Family stories, lessons, letters, decisions — each told in its own voice
Each type has its own form
A family story form asks for the narrator, the year, the place, and asks you to tell it fully — not summarise it. A letter form asks who it is to, when to open it, and gives you space to say the things you have always wanted to say. A decision principle form asks for the rule, a real example, and what happens when it is broken.
📜 Family story💡 Business / life lesson✉️ Letter to future🎯 Decision principle🗺️ Migration / origin
Finance & business
Property, insurance, gold, bank accounts, investments — each with its own fields
Each type has its own form
A property form captures registration number, exact document location, and instructions for future generations. An insurance form has the policy number, premium due date, agent contact, and claim procedure step by step. Emergency & operations
Built for crisis — each emergency type has a different structure
Each type has its own form
A medical emergency card is a single summary: blood group, allergies, conditions, medications, doctor number, hospital, and insurance — everything a paramedic needs in 30 seconds. A document location entry tells exactly which shelf, drawer, and file. A password hint uses a memory clue, never the actual password. An SOP entry is numbered steps for someone in panic.
🏥 Medical emergency📁 Document location🔑 Password hint⚡ Utility / home📋 SOP / instruction
Media & memories

A handwritten letter form preserves author, recipient, year, a full transcription, and why it must be kept. A photo album form identifies who appears on every key page and where the album is stored.
